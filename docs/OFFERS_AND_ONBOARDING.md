# Offers and onboarding

People → Hiring and onboarding has four tabs: **Offered**, **Accepted**, **Onboarding**
and **Revoked**. Status moves one way: offer → accepted → onboarding → active.

## Offer

Issuing an offer records the person at *Offer* with the offered salary and prints the
offer letter. The joining date is optional; without one the letter says it will be
confirmed in the joining letter. Mark the offer **accepted** when the candidate agrees.

## Revoke

An offer can be revoked while it is offered or accepted, with a reason. The person
leaves the pipeline and every list; the offer, its letter and the reason stay on record
under **Revoked**. Once onboarding has started an offer cannot be revoked.

## Onboard

From **Accepted**, onboard one person (**Onboard**) or several (**Onboard selected**)
with one joining date. For each person, all together or none:

- the joining date is set (on the person and the offer);
- the offered salary starts from that date;
- the joining letter is issued, stating the date and salary;
- the person moves to *Onboarding* with the Pay and Joining letter steps done.

A joining date cannot be before the offer was issued. Someone without a joining date
cannot be onboarded; the database refuses a person at onboarding or later without one.

## Formalities and activation

Onboarding is finished on the person's profile, step by step: personal details, PAN
and Aadhaar, bank account, pay, statutory setup and inductions. **Activate** unlocks
once every required step is done. An active employee registers their face and punches
at any site, and appears in attendance from the joining date and in the next payroll.
