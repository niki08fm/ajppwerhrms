## Watch or save to Google Drive

1. Download and extract the ZIP.
2. Open `AJPower-training-with-audio/index.html` to search the lessons and watch them locally, or open the numbered MP4s directly from `videos/`.
3. Upload the MP4s to your Google Drive folder. Keep their numbered filenames so they stay in order.
4. Turn on the video player sound and set your device volume. English narration is included in each MP4.
5. Use the PDF alongside the videos. Optional English SRT captions and narration transcripts are included; instructions are also visible on screen.
